> **In short.** Start with a repetitive, high-volume process with clear rules: answering common questions, pulling data from invoices, routing requests. That's where AI saves hours every week and mistakes are easy to check. An automation project starts at €5,000 and launches with a person approving every result.

## What AI automation means in practice

A program receives something (an email, a PDF, a message), understands what's in it and takes a step: fills in a table, drafts a reply, routes the request to the right person. Unlike classic automation, it can handle free text, not just forms.

## 9 processes to start with

| # | Process | What the AI does | Typical saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common customer questions | Drafts answers from company documents | Hours a day |
| 2 | Invoices and receipts | Extracts supplier, amount, date, VAT | Hours a week |
| 3 | Request routing | Reads the email and sends it to the right team | Minutes per request |
| 4 | Proposals from templates | Fills the proposal from call notes | 30–60 min each |
| 5 | Call summaries | Notes and next steps from a recording | 15 min per call |
| 6 | Contract search | Answers with a quote from the source | Hours per search |
| 7 | Product descriptions | Copy from specs, in 2–3 languages | Hours per catalogue |
| 8 | Data checks | Flags missing or unusual fields | Errors caught early |
| 9 | Weekly reports | Summary text from existing numbers | 1–2 hours per report |

## How to pick the first process

Ask three questions about each one:

1. **How often does it happen?** Daily beats monthly.
2. **How easy is the result to check?** An invoice total takes 5 seconds to verify.
3. **What does a mistake cost?** A wrong answer to a simple question costs little. A wrong contract costs a lot.

High frequency, easy checking and low cost of error make the first candidate.

## Risks and how to control them

### Made-up answers
The model answers only from your documents and cites the source. If it finds nothing, it says so.

### Confidential data
Data stays in your accounts. We use model providers that don't train on your data.

### Runaway costs
Every request has a cost. We set a daily and per-user cap, visible in the panel.

### Team trust
For the first weeks, a person approves every result. Full automation comes only once the correction rate drops.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does AI automation cost?
A project starts at €5,000. Running costs depend on volume and are usually tens of euros a month for a small business.

### Does my data need to be organised?
It helps but isn't required. The first step is gathering documents and seeing what's missing.

### Does AI replace employees?
In the processes above, it takes over the repetitive part. People stay on checks, exceptions and customer relationships.
