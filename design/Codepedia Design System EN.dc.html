Rule
Signal is never used for body text: the contrast is too low. Only section numbers, underlines and button fills.
Focus
Global: outline 2px signal, offset 2px. No default blue ring.
Radii and shadows
One radius: 4px (rounded). No shadows: surfaces use a hairline border.
